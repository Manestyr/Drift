All WAV files in this directory are original, procedurally synthesized DRIFT assets.
Dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
No recordings, samples, external assets or third-party dependencies were used.
Reproduce with `python3 tools/generate_audio.py` (standard library only).
Mono 22,050 Hz / signed 16-bit PCM. Per-file peak clamped to 0.8;
short sounds have attack/release envelopes. Engine has integer-cycle loop boundaries.
